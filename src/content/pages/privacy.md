We are a volunteer-run community, not a company, and we collect as little as we can. This page says plainly what that means.

## This website

This site has **no forms, no accounts and no tracking cookies**, and it does not load advertising or social-media scripts. The only thing stored in your browser is your light or dark theme choice, if you make one; it stays on your device and we never see it.

The site is served by Amazon Web Services. As with any web host, your device's address is used to send you the pages. We do not collect or keep it.

If we ever add visitor counting, it will be cookieless and anonymous, and this page will be updated first.

## Registration and tickets

Registration is handled by Konfhub, and sometimes Meetup. The details you give when you register are held by them, under their own privacy policies. We receive attendee lists for the event you registered for, and we use them only to run that event, such as check-in and venue security. We do not sell or share them.

## Contacting us

If you email us, we use your message only to reply to you. We keep it as long as we need to deal with it.

## Photos

We take photos at events and publish a small selection, mostly crowd shots. We never tag people by name, and we remove location and device data from every photo before it goes online.

- Ask for a **no-photo lanyard** at registration, or tell any organizer, if you would rather not be photographed.
- If you appear in a published photo and want it removed, email us from the [contact page](/contact/) with the subject "Photo removal" and tell us which photo. We will take it down promptly.

## Speakers

We publish a speaker's name, photo, profile links and talks. A speaker's photo and their own page appear only with their permission. Speakers can ask us to hide their photo or page at any time.

## Your rights

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, gives you rights over your personal data, including to ask what we hold about you and to have it corrected or erased. Write to us from the [contact page](/contact/) and we will respond as promptly as we can. We may need to confirm who you are first.

## Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, we will update the date below. Questions are welcome on the [contact page](/contact/).
