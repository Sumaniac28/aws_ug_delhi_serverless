AWS User Group Delhi NCR is a community of people who like learning together. Our events should be welcoming, safe and useful for everyone, whatever their background, identity, experience or job title. This code applies to everyone: attendees, speakers, sponsors, volunteers and organizers, at our events, online sessions, chats and social channels.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and respectful.** Listen, assume good faith, and give people room to ask basic questions. Everyone here was a beginner once.
- **Keep it constructive.** Disagree with ideas, not people. Give feedback the way you would like to receive it.
- **Respect privacy.** Ask before photographing someone close up, and respect a no-photo lanyard or a request to delete a picture.
- **Look after the space.** Follow the venue's rules, and tell an organizer if something is unsafe.
- **Share the floor.** Do not dominate Q&A, and make room for quieter voices.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, bullying, discrimination or intimidation of any kind, including because of gender, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, caste, religion, age, language or region.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, comments or images, and sexualised content in talks or slides.
- Deliberate misgendering, insults, threats, or personal or professional attacks.
- Stalking, following, or sustained, unwanted contact.
- Recording or sharing someone's image or words after they have asked you not to.
- Disrupting talks or conversations, or pushing sales pitches on attendees who have not asked for them.
- Encouraging or helping anyone else to do any of the above.

## Speakers, sponsors and recruiters

Talks, slides, demos and booth material follow this code too. Sponsors and recruiters are guests of our community: introduce yourself, respect a "no thanks", and do not collect attendees' details without their consent.

## Photos and recordings

We take photos at events and publish a small selection, mostly crowd shots. We never tag attendees. If you do not want to be photographed, ask for a no-photo lanyard at registration, or tell any organizer. To have a published photo removed, write to us from the [contact page](/contact/) and we will take it down promptly.

## If something happens

If you experience or see behaviour that breaks this code, please tell us. You can:

1. Talk to any organizer at the event. They wear organizer badges, and will listen without judgement.
2. Email us from the [contact page](/contact/) with the subject "Code of Conduct". Say what happened, when and where, who was involved, and whether anyone saw it. You may report anonymously, though we may not be able to follow up.

Reports are handled in confidence and shared only with the organizers who need to act on them. We will never ask you to confront the person involved.

## What we will do

Organizers will act quickly and fairly. Depending on what happened, we may ask someone to stop, give a warning, ask them to leave the event, remove them from our channels, or decline their future participation. We may also tell the venue or, where someone is in danger, the authorities. Anyone asked to stop is expected to do so straight away.

We will not retaliate against, or tolerate retaliation towards, anyone who reports in good faith.

## Beyond our events

Our events are held at venues and with partners that have their own rules, and we also follow the [AWS Code of Conduct](https://aws.amazon.com/codeofconduct/). Where the two differ, the stricter one applies.

## Changes

We review this code as the community grows. Suggestions are welcome through the [contact page](/contact/). This page shows when it was last updated.
