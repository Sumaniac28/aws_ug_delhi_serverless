AWS User Group Delhi NCR is a **volunteer-run community** for people who build on AWS: developers, architects, students and curious beginners across Delhi, Gurugram and Noida.

We run:

- **Meetups** with a few talks and time to network.
- **Workshops and online sessions** for smaller, hands-on learning.
- **Community Day**, our yearly flagship conference with tracks, workshops and sponsors.

Everything here is run by volunteers in their spare time. Registration and tickets are handled on Konfhub, and announcements go out on Meetup and our social channels.
